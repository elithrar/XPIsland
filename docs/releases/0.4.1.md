XPIsland 0.4.1 for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 only.

- Hearthstones, zoning and dungeon loading screens preserve the session and count as gameplay downtime in XP/hour and time-to-level calculations.
- XP reads wait until the world is available. A transient zero or out-of-order level update no longer permanently disables estimates or turns restored XP into a duplicate award.
- Unknown level thresholds or a confirmed correction preserve recorded counters and restart only the rolling pace history. The temporary tooltip says “Recalculating your leveling pace.” Normal travel shows no warning.
- Old sessions marked incomplete recover on normal addon restoration without clearing their recorded XP. Actual disconnections and reload downtime retain their separate handling.

Regression tests cover loading-event order, a blocked-renderer load, unavailable unit values, dungeon boundaries, delayed rollover, the final level, genuine corrections and 0.3/0.4 session migration. The exact live event sequence behind the report was not captured; the failure path was reproduced against 0.4.0 and fixed offline.
